about

notes on attention, memory, and atmosphere

Fog and Order is a small collection of photographs, books, notes, and passing observations.

I made this site as a slower alternative to social media — a place to keep track of the things that continue to stay with me.

Most of the photographs are taken while walking without much of a plan. The reading list is made up of books that changed the way I see, think, or pay attention.

I’m interested in atmosphere, memory, cities, systems, and the quiet structure hidden inside ordinary life.

New work and occasional thoughts are shared through the subscriber page.

thanks for visiting.
